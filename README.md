# My Favorite Series: Stranger Things

## Overview
**Stranger Things** is a sci-fi horror series set in *Hawkins, Indiana* during the 1980s. It follows a group of kids who uncover secret government experiments and a dark parallel world called the **Upside Down**.

## Main Characters
- Eleven
- Mike Wheeler
- Dustin Henderson
- Lucas Sinclair
- Will Byers

## Why I Like It
1. The 80s setting and soundtrack
2. Strong friendships between the characters
3. A good mix of mystery, horror, and comedy

> Friends don't lie.
